Cultural & Vocational Pipeline

The Heritage Hub & Cultural Enterprise

A self-sustaining social enterprise training young people in traditional handicrafts and performing arts. Young people manage and execute cultural displays for corporate and community hire, a genuine pre-employment incubator in business management, logistics, and financial discipline.

  • Textiles, design & hair braiding craft training
  • Performing arts & cultural display production
  • Youth-led business management, logistics & financial discipline
  • Corporate and community hire of cultural showcases
Retention & Workforce Readiness

The SOS Mental Fortitude Framework

Our proprietary, evidence-based Support, Observe, Succeed (SOS) framework delivers clinical counselling and peer-led workshops that build psychological safety at every stage of a young person's journey.

SOS systematically dismantles the mental barriers, severe anxiety, trauma, and social isolation that most often derail education and employment. The result is measurably higher workforce and education retention, and reduced local public spend on mental health crises.

  • Clinical, certified counselling provision
  • Peer-led emotional regulation workshops
  • Trauma-informed group therapy models
  • Structured retention tracking across education & employment
