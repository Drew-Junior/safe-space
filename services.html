A structured pipeline from potential to opportunity

Every service at Safe Space - Notts CIC exists to move a young person from psychological readiness to certified skill, to sustained economic participation.

Cultural & Vocational Pipeline

The Heritage Hub & Cultural Enterprise

A vibrant creative hub where culture, skill, and opportunity meet. The Heritage Hub trains young people in textiles, hair artistry, and performance, while grounding them in the practical disciplines that shape real-world success, business management, logistics, and financial responsibility.

This is more than a programme. It is a pipeline that transforms heritage into confidence, confidence into competence, and competence into employability. Young people leave with:

  • Tangible vocational skills
  • A portfolio that reflects their creativity
  • Entrepreneurial readiness
  • A clear pathway into work or self-employment

The Heritage Hub is designed to be self-sustaining, nurturing creators, cultural leaders, and young entrepreneurs who carry their identity with pride.

Retention & Readiness

The SOS Mental Fortitude Framework

A trauma-informed, relational model that strengthens emotional resilience from the inside out. Built around the Support, Observe, Succeed (SOS) methodology, this pillar offers clinical counselling, peer-led circles, and mental fortitude workshops that help young people dismantle anxiety, isolation, and emotional overload.

This is not traditional therapy, it is mental fortitude training. A structured pathway that teaches young people to:

  • Recognise their internal weather
  • Regulate emotional storms
  • Build coping strategies
  • Strengthen identity
  • Stay connected to the community

The SOS Framework is the emotional engine of Safe Space, the part that keeps young people grounded, seen, and ready to grow.

Early Years Governance & Scalability

Foundational Learning, Built to Grow

A dedicated early years model designed to evolve from small-group childcare into full-service nursery provision. This pillar establishes the systems that protect children's learning, safety, and emotional development, governance, quality assurance, and scalable routines that can be replicated across multiple communities. It builds:

  • Strong safeguarding practice
  • Consistent daily rhythms
  • Developmental tracking
  • Staff training pathways
  • A curriculum that blends cultural identity with early learning standards

This is the foundation of Safe Space's long-term vision: a model that grows with families, strengthens communities, and sets children on a path of confident, culturally rooted learning.

Family & Community Support

Weekly Community Programme

Alongside our core pillars, Safe Space runs a weekly rhythm of drop-in sessions open to parents, carers, fathers, and children across the community, low-barrier, welcoming spaces built around connection as much as content.

Daily-10:30am

Parents' Chit Chat

A relaxed, informal space for parents and carers to sit down, talk, and be heard. No agenda beyond connection, a cup of tea, a listening ear, and the chance to share the week with people who understand.

OngoingBy arrangement

Children's Drop-In Centre

A flexible, always-welcoming space for children to come, play, and be safely supervised in a warm and structured environment. Whether for an hour or an afternoon, children are met with the same consistency and care as every other Safe Space programme.

Friday12:00pm

Dad's Life Matters

A dedicated space for fathers to connect, talk openly, and be recognised as a vital part of family life. Dad's Life Matters creates room for honest conversation, peer support, and practical guidance — because a father's wellbeing shapes the whole family's.

Thursday11:00am

Family Learning

Parents and children learning side by side, building everyday skills, literacy, numeracy, and confidence through shared, hands-on activity. A weekly reminder that learning is something families can grow together, not something that happens to a child alone.
