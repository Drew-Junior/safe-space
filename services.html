Services

A structured pipeline from potential to opportunity

Every service at Safe Space - Notts CIC exists to move a young person from psychological readiness, to certified skill, to sustained economic participation.

01 The Economic Pipeline

The Cook School & Artisanal Enterprise

A professional culinary arts and hospitality training pipeline equipping underrepresented, vulnerable, and NEET young people with certified, market-ready skills matched to Nottingham's hospitality demand.

Alongside it, the Heritage Hub & Cultural Enterprise is a self-sustaining social enterprise training youth in traditional handicrafts and performing arts. Young people manage and execute cultural displays for corporate and community hire — a genuine pre-employment incubator in business management, logistics, and financial discipline.

  • Certified culinary arts & hospitality service training
  • Textiles, design & hair braiding craft training
  • Performing arts & cultural display production
  • Youth-led business management, logistics & financial discipline
  • Corporate and community hire of cultural showcases
02 Retention & Workforce Readiness

The SOS Mental Fortitude Framework

Our proprietary, evidence-based Support, Observe, Succeed (SOS) framework delivers clinical counselling and peer-led workshops that build psychological safety at every stage of a young person's journey.

SOS systematically dismantles the mental barriers — severe anxiety, trauma, social isolation — that most often derail education and employment. The result is measurably higher workforce and education retention, and reduced local public spend on mental health crises.

  • Clinical, certified counselling provision
  • Peer-led emotional regulation workshops
  • Trauma-informed group therapy models
  • Structured retention tracking across education & employment
03 Multigenerational Scalability

Early Years Governance

A dedicated childcare model designed from first principles to scale into full-service nursery provision — securing foundational early years learning standards from the ground up, and extending the Safe Space mission across generations.

  • Statutory-compliant early years childcare model
  • Governance framework built for full nursery scale-up
  • Foundational learning standards from the ground up
