Visionary & Pastoral Lead

Reverend Minister Nancy Abakah Philips leads the overall strategic direction of Safe Space - Notts CIC, driving regional mobilisation across Nottinghamshire and holding personal responsibility for the organisation's pastoral care execution. Her leadership grounds Safe Space's dual identity: a rigorous, regulated youth development platform carried by a genuine sense of calling and community stewardship.
